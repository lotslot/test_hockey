Neutral Zone · September 14, 2026

Chicago Mission Labor Day 14’s: 83 Evaluations

Ryan Lee #97 (F, R, 5’9”, 141, LA Jr. Kings U14, 01/04/2012) – Lee is a smooth skating winger that can both facilitate and finish from the wing as he plays with pace and a fluid stride with easy edgework to evade defenders and push tempo.

His hands are firm but have soft and subtle touches on them where he can dangle off the rush. Away from the puck he presents well, getting to scoring areas and is always ready to let it rip. Off the rush Lee can get to the net or play as a trailer, sensing the spacing to be the best option for a scoring position.

Neutral Zone · June 27, 2026

Western Region High Performance Camp 14U: 83 Players Evaluated

Ryan Lee, who will join the LA Jr. Kings after spending last season with the Anaheim Jr. Ducks, is a dynamic, high-tempo forward whose game is built around explosiveness, offensive confidence, and relentless pace.

His skating is his defining attribute. He possesses outstanding burst speed and explodes into open space, allowing him to separate from defenders and create scoring opportunities in transition. He constantly keeps his feet moving, searching for seams and attacking lanes with confidence.

His combination of skating ability and offensive instincts makes him a dangerous transition player with significant upside. Continuing to stay emotionally even throughout games will allow him to maintain his pace and confidence regardless of the scoreboard.

Western Regional Select 14 High Performance Camp

Lee’s versatility is what allowed him to be one of the more effective forwards on Team Grey. He was a slippery attacker with the puck on his stick, flashing the ability to dodge around checks at top speed to work his way around defenders before connecting on a cross-ice feed to the middle to create a dangerous scoring chance for his linemates.
